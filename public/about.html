About Veritas

Build stronger arguments through practice.

Veritas is an online platform for learning and practising debate. It gives learners a place to shape arguments, test ideas, and grow their confidence with structured practice and useful feedback.

Why Veritas exists

Good debate is more than having a quick answer. It takes clear reasoning, reliable evidence, and the ability to understand another point of view. Veritas makes it easier to practise those skills regularly, reflect on the result, and see where to improve.

The Evidence Lab supports this goal by helping learners break a claim into questions, explore relevant sources, and compare evidence from different perspectives. It is designed to encourage careful thinking about information behind an argument.

Ways to learn and compete

Debate the AI

Practise making a case in a guided debate, then use feedback to strengthen your next argument.

Evidence Lab

Investigate a claim, review sources, and compare supporting and opposing evidence.

Study and daily challenges

Build knowledge and practise specific debate skills with topic study and short recurring challenges.

Live 1v1 and Classroom

Debate real opponents, track your progress, or work through assignments and feedback in a classroom.
